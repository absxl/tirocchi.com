Reframing the idea of dying of thirst as a full-blown convenience-store attack.

## Contesto

Thirsty Attack turns Liquid Death into the centre of a zombie outbreak: a campaign world built around a can, a convenience store and a playable visual language.

## Insight

When thirst takes over, the product becomes the only thing that matters.

## Idea

Build a horror-comedy universe where the desperate search for water drives the story, the characters and the visual system.

## Sviluppo

A campaign developed across a hero spot, a strategic board and a modular animated visual system.

## Il mio ruolo

Progetto e art direction di Matteo Tirocchi e Ginevra Di Foggia; copywriting di Rebecca Pierucci e Alida Di Gaetano.
